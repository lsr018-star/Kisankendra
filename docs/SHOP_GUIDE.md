# Kisankendra — Partner Shop Owner Guide

Login with your shop email/mobile at **Shop Login**. Your ERP opens at `/shop`.

## Getting started

1. Register your shop (home page → Register Your Shop). Status starts
   **Pending**.
2. Kisankendra verifies your shop and licences. Only **Approved +
   licence-verified** shops receive platform orders.
3. Your dashboard shows today's numbers: customers, orders, sales, pending
   orders and low-stock alerts — all computed from your real data.

## Working with platform orders (Orders tab)

- New orders arrive as **Pending**. Check stock and price, then **Accept**
  or **Reject**. If you reject, the order is offered to the next eligible
  shop automatically.
- Move accepted orders forward as you work: Processing → Ready for Dispatch
  → Out for Delivery → **Delivered**. Stock is reserved when the order is
  placed and leaves your shelf on delivery, so you cannot oversell.
- Every status change records who did it and when (visible to the customer
  as tracking).

## Customers (Customers tab + Customer 360)

- Add walk-in customers manually; search by name, mobile or customer ID.
- Each profile shows, separately: **(1)** Kisankendra platform orders,
  **(2)** manual orders you entered, **(3)** assigned orders not yet
  completed — plus total purchase value, notes and support history.
- Record marketing consent only when the customer actually agrees; the date
  is stored. Promotional messages must respect consent.
- You only ever see **your own shop's** customers. Another shop's records
  are not visible to you, and yours are not visible to them.
- **Record Walk-in Purchase** on a customer profile creates a manual order
  and reduces stock immediately.

## Inventory (Inventory tab)

- Review stock, reserved quantities, batch and expiry. Rows highlighted red
  are at or below the low-stock threshold.
- Adjust stock with **+/− and a reason** — every adjustment is written to
  the stock-movement audit history.
- Purchase cost is confidential: visible to you (owner), hidden from staff
  accounts without the finance permission.

## Reminders (Reminders tab)

Create follow-ups for customers, stock replenishment, licence renewal and
service tasks; tick them off when done. Customer follow-up reminders can
also be created from the customer profile.

## Staff accounts

Staff log in with their own accounts and see only the permissions you are
granted by the platform setup (demo staff: orders + customers, no cost
prices, no stock adjustment).

## Inventory batches & expiry (Phase 2)

Every stock line is one **batch** (batch number + expiry date). The same
product/pack can be held as several batches. Use **Inventory → Add batch**
for new stock (this is also how a newly approved shop starts), edit a
batch's expiry/prices from the same screen, and use stock adjustments for
corrections (a reason is recorded). The dashboard shows **expiry alerts**:
batches expiring within 45 days are flagged amber; expired batches are
flagged red and can never be sold — orders always consume the
earliest-expiring valid batch first (FEFO).

## Reports (Phase 2)

**Reports** gives three views over any date range, with **CSV download**:

- **Sales** — orders and gross sales, plus per-product quantities.
- **Margin** — per-order supply cost, commission and margin (owner, or
  staff with the finance permission).
- **Reconciliation** — what was expected vs collected vs outstanding,
  order by order.

## Subscription (Phase 2)

**Subscription** shows your current plan, its status (Active / Grace /
Expired), and billing history. To change plan, pick one and either record a
payment claim (UPI reference or COD note — an admin confirms it) or pay via
the payment gateway once the platform enables it.

## Analytics & settlements (Phase 3)

**Analytics** shows your last-30-days revenue trend, top products, top
villages, repeat-customer rate and the order funnel. **Settlements** lists
the platform's period settlements for your shop (gross, commission, supply
amount due) once the admin generates them.
