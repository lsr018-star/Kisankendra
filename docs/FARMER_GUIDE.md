# Kisankendra — Farmer Guide

**Kisan ka Saathi, Har Khet ke Liye.**

## Create your account

1. Tap **Farmer Login** → **Register**. Enter your name, mobile number,
   village and a password (6+ characters).
2. Next time, log in with your mobile number (or email) and password.

## Order products

1. Open **Products**. Search by name, brand or crop, or filter by category
   (Pesticides / Fertilizers), brand, price and availability.
2. Open a product to see pack sizes, price, stock, and the label information
   and warnings. Always follow the printed label when using any product.
3. **Add to Cart**, open the Cart, check the price summary (delivery fee and
   tax are shown before you confirm).
4. Enter your name, mobile, village and address, choose **Home Delivery** or
   **Field Delivery**, pick Cash on Delivery, and **Place Order**.
5. Your order is assigned to an authorised partner shop near you that has
   the stock. The shop's name appears on your order — that shop is your
   seller, and its details are on your invoice.

## Track, reorder, cancel

- **My Dashboard → My Orders** lists everything. Open an order for the full
  tracking timeline.
- **Reorder** puts a past order's items back in your cart.
- You can **Cancel** an order that has not gone out for delivery. If no shop
  can fulfil an order, you will see a clear message — you can wait for our
  team to assign a shop, or cancel at no cost.

## Crop help

- **Solution Box** (home page) or **Crop Support** (in your account):
  describe the crop, stage and symptoms, and attach a photo of the affected
  plant (under 500 KB). Our support team responds in your account.
- **Disease Finder** (home page) is a reference library — helpful for
  learning, but it is **not a confirmed diagnosis**.

## Language

Use the **English / हिन्दी** selector at the top at any time. Your choice is
remembered on your device and saved to your profile.

## Your home screen — three big buttons

After login, your dashboard starts with three large buttons:

- **Order Again (फिर से ऑर्डर करें)** — your orders, with one-tap reorder.
- **Track Order (ऑर्डर देखें)** — goes straight to your current order's
  tracking page.
- **Help for Crop (फसल में मदद)** — ask for crop help. You can attach a
  photo of the affected plant (choose the photo, check the preview, then
  submit); the photo is saved with your request and shown to the expert
  who replies.
